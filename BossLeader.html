P3_Introduction: Difference between Boss and Leader

We think that bosses and leaders are different role.
For example, difference of them are "Control or Lead".
Bosses control team members with fear or authority.
Leaders lead by trust and by setting an example, encouraging members to grow and think for themselves.
So, bosses gives orders and expects obedience, while leaders listens and shares a clear vision.
Next, We will show 3 examples.

P4_社会的な部分 (Social Aspect)

Bosses and Leader in Social.
If team member make mistakes, Bosses say "You missed".
But, leader says "You missed, I were missed, too. What is bad?"
So, Boss passes the buck to team members.
Next, where does power come from?
A boss's power comes from their title. The company gives it to them.
A leader's power comes from trust. People give it to them.

P5_国的な部分 (National Aspect)

(Boss is the Emperor, Leader is the Prime Minister)
Japan does not have a person called a “boss.” However, Japan has an Emperor. The Emperor is a symbol of Japan. He does not do politics. He takes part in important events and meets foreign ambassadors.
Japan also has a leader called the Prime Minister. The Prime Minister is Sanae Takaichi. She makes important decisions for Japan and works to make life better for the people. She also talks with leaders from other countries and thinks about Japan’s safety.

P6_学校的な部分 (School Aspect)

Boss and Leader at School
Next, I will talk about the difference between a boss and a leader, using school as an example.
For example, imagine a class cleaning time.
A boss tells students what to do.
They might say, “You clean the classroom. You clean the hallway.”
The boss gives orders, but does not always help the students.
On the other hand, a leader works with the students.
For example, a leader might say, “Let’s clean the classroom together.”
The leader helps others and listens to their ideas.
So, I think a boss makes people follow, but a leader makes people want to work together.

P7_まとめ (Conclusion)

To summarize, bosses and leaders are very different.
Bosses use their title to control people. They pass the buck when things go wrong.
But leaders use trust to guide people. They work together and help everyone grow.
In society, in Japan, and at school, we can see these differences.
I think it is important to be a good leader, not just a boss.
